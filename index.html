End-to-End System Design & The Deterministic Engine

A resilient, enterprise-grade architecture that decouples semantic natural language parsing from mathematical business logic.

01

First-Level Intent Classifier

The moment an unstructured email arrives, the semantic layer categorizes it into one of 4 operational archetypes: Wedding, Corporate Offsite, Conference, or Family Reunion. This immediately sets the occupancy density rules and cross-sell tree path.

02

The "Rule of 3" Gatekeeper

Only 3 non-negotiable anchors are required for Quote #1: Guest Count, Dates/Duration, and Booking Type. If present, quote immediately. Questions about room tier, catering, and specific halls are handled via stated assumptions rather than stalling with extra email turns.

03

Deterministic Rules Engine

Zero hallucinated math. Prices, room inventory checks, volume discounts (20+ rooms $\to$ 10%), advance booking discounts (60+ days $\to$ 5%), and inverse budget solvers are executed by pure JavaScript code, ensuring 100% auditability.

04

Transparent Brain Inspector

Every response surfaces an auditable trace: parsed entities, missing items, assumptions made, rules fired, and formula math. Hotel staff and guests can instantly see exactly how every single rupee was computed.
